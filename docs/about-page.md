Store unreconstituted lyophilized (freeze-dried) peptide powder in a freezer at −20°C or below for long-term stability. Lyophilized peptides remain structurally stable under ambient transit conditions due to the inherent resilience of the lyophilization process. Once reconstituted with bacteriostatic water, refrigerate at 2–8°C and use within 2–4 weeks.
