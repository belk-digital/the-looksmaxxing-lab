10. FAQs
What is MOTS-C?
MOTS-C is a 16-amino-acid mitochondrial-derived peptide (MDP) encoded by mitochondrial DNA. It is studied for its proposed role in cellular energy regulation, metabolic signaling, and exercise-linked physiological adaptation, making it one of the most actively researched compounds in mitochondrial biology today.
How does MOTS-C work?
Research suggests MOTS-C functions as a signaling peptide that travels from the mitochondria to the nucleus, where it may influence AMPK pathway activation and downstream metabolic gene expression. This mitochondrial-to-nuclear communication is central to current hypotheses about its role in energy homeostasis.
What is a mitochondrial-derived peptide?
A mitochondrial-derived peptide is a small protein fragment encoded within mitochondrial DNA rather than nuclear DNA. These peptides, including MOTS-C, are believed to participate in cell-to-cell and organelle-to-nucleus signaling related to metabolism, stress response, and cellular aging.
Why is MOTS-C important in metabolic research?
MOTS-C is studied for its potential connection to metabolic regulation, mitochondrial biogenesis, and exercise physiology. Researchers investigating healthy aging, insulin sensitivity pathways, and cellular energy production frequently include MOTS-C in comparative experimental models.
What are MOTS-C's primary research applications?
Current research applications include mitochondrial signaling studies, AMPK pathway investigation, skeletal muscle metabolism models, and longevity-focused cellular aging research. It is also used in comparative studies against other mitochondrial-derived peptides such as Humanin and SS-31.
How is MOTS-C purity verified?
Purity is verified through preparative High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) during purification and confirmed via Electrospray Ionization Mass Spectrometry (ESI-MS) for final molecular weight identification. Each batch is documented with a Certificate of Analysis (COA) prior to release.
What testing standards does this MOTS-C meet?
Our MOTS-C 10mg is synthesized via Solid-Phase Peptide Synthesis, purified to remove truncated and deletion sequences, and verified to exceed 99% purity. Every batch is independently lab-tested and ships with COA documentation for full traceability.
What is included in a Certificate of Analysis?
A Certificate of Analysis (COA) documents the verified purity percentage, molecular weight confirmation via mass spectrometry, batch number, and synthesis date, allowing researchers to trace each vial back to its specific manufacturing lot.
How should MOTS-C be stored?
Lyophilized MOTS-C should be stored at 2–8°C, protected from light and moisture, until reconstitution. Researchers should follow standard peptide-handling protocols for laboratory storage to preserve molecular stability across longitudinal studies.
Is MOTS-C approved for human use?
No. MOTS-C is sold strictly for research and laboratory use only. It has not been evaluated or approved by the FDA or any other regulatory body for human consumption, veterinary application, or therapeutic use of any kind.
