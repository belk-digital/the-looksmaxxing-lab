10. FAQs
What is Glutathione 200mg?
Glutathione 200mg is a research-grade, reduced-form antioxidant compound studied for its central role in cellular antioxidant defense and redox balance. It is manufactured to strict purity standards and intended exclusively for scientific and laboratory research applications.
What is Reduced Glutathione?
Reduced glutathione is the biologically active form of glutathione, distinguished by an intact thiol group that allows it to donate electrons and neutralize free radicals. It is the form most relevant to antioxidant and redox biology research.
How does Glutathione work?
Research indicates glutathione functions as the body's primary intracellular antioxidant, neutralizing reactive oxygen species and supporting detoxification pathways by donating electrons through its thiol group, which becomes oxidized in the process and can later be regenerated.
What research applications does Glutathione have?
Glutathione is studied in research contexts involving oxidative stress, free radical scavenging, cellular detoxification pathways, and redox homeostasis, often in comparative models alongside other antioxidant compounds.
How is Glutathione tested for purity?
Purity is verified through preparative High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) during purification and confirmed via Electrospray Ionization Mass Spectrometry (ESI-MS) for final molecular weight identification. Each batch is documented with a Certificate of Analysis (COA) prior to release.
What quality standards are followed?
Our Glutathione 200mg is purified to isolate the reduced form, remove oxidized variants, and verified to exceed 99% purity. Every batch is independently lab-tested and ships with COA documentation for full traceability.
What is a Certificate of Analysis?
A Certificate of Analysis (COA) documents the verified purity percentage, molecular weight confirmation via mass spectrometry, batch number, and synthesis date, allowing researchers to trace each vial back to its specific manufacturing lot.
How should Glutathione be stored?
Lyophilized glutathione should be stored at 2–8°C, protected from light and moisture, until reconstitution. Researchers should follow standard handling protocols for laboratory storage to preserve molecular stability and prevent premature oxidation.
Why is third-party testing important?
Third-party or independent verification confirms that a compound's purity and identity claims are accurate and not self-reported, giving researchers confidence that experimental results reflect the true reduced-form content rather than oxidized contamination.
Is Glutathione intended for human use?
No. Glutathione is sold strictly for research and laboratory use only. It has not been evaluated or approved by the FDA or any other regulatory body for human consumption, veterinary application, or therapeutic use of any kind.
