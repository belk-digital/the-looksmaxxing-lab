Q5. What is Selank?
Selank is a synthetic heptapeptide derived from tuftsin, an immunomodulatory tetrapeptide, with a C-terminal Pro-Gly-Pro extension that enhances enzymatic stability. It is one of the most referenced neuropeptides in GABAergic signaling and neurotransmitter regulation research in laboratory models.
Q6. Is Selank a nootropic peptide?
Selank is widely classified as a nootropic neuropeptide in research literature due to its studied interactions with GABAergic neurotransmission, enkephalinase inhibition, and serotonin metabolism in laboratory models. It is strictly for in-vitro and non-human research use only.
